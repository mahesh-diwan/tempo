## Extraction notes

- Anonymous `visitorData` is required. `InnerTube::fetch_visitor_data` is called once before the
  direct-client loop; without it `VISIONOS` answers `UNPLAYABLE` and the Android VR clients answer
  `LOGIN_REQUIRED`.
- `VISIONOS` and `ANDROID_VR_1_65_10` return `OK` after visitorData. `ANDROID_VR_1_43_32` still
  answered `LOGIN_REQUIRED` on the sampled tracks.
- `IOS` is not used: its googlevideo URLs serve only bounded-Range requests.
- rustypipe was not needed for any track in this run. Its IOS URLs would need a Range-rewriting
  proxy to play in mpv, so it remains a fallback only.
